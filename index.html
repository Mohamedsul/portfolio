About

I bridge business
problems and data.

Most organizations don't have a data problem — they have a decision problem. The data exists, but it's scattered across systems, unreconciled, or arrives too late to change anything. My practice is built around closing that gap.

I work at the intersection of business analysis and data engineering — understanding what decision actually needs to be made, then building the SQL pipelines, Power BI dashboards, and KPI frameworks that make it possible to make that decision faster and with more confidence.

I'm not simply someone who builds dashboards. My engagements span public-sector programmes, retail, fintech, logistics, and enterprise SaaS — the goal is always the same: insight that a stakeholder can act on, not a report that sits unread.

Decision-first

Every engagement starts with the decision, not the dashboard. I map where data currently fails to inform action before proposing any solution.

End-to-end delivery

From data discovery through analysis, visualization, and adoption. No handoff ambiguity — I own the insight until it's actually in use.

KPI accountability

Every dashboard or model ships tied to a metric the business already tracks — revenue, cost, efficiency, or risk. Not vanity accuracy.

01 — Retail / E-commerce
Customer Retention Analytics
Predictive Churn Modeling · CLV Analysis
35%
Churn rate reduced
Business Challenge

A mid-market retailer was losing 18% of customers annually with no early-warning system. Retention spend was reactive, burning margin without improving outcomes.

Decision Gap

Commercial teams had no visibility into at-risk accounts until cancellation — the intervention window closed 4–6 weeks before anyone knew there was a problem.

Analysis & Solution

Built a churn model on behavioral and transaction data, reaching 92% accuracy at a 30-day horizon, and integrated the scores into the CRM to drive segment-specific interventions automatically.

Recommendation

Chose an interpretable model over a marginally more accurate black box, so the commercial team could act on scores directly without data-science mediation each time.

Key Insight: The biggest lever wasn't model accuracy — it was cutting the lag between signal and action. Connecting predictions to CRM workflows generated roughly 3× the impact of any further model tuning.

02 — Financial Services
Fraud & Risk Analytics
Anomaly Detection · Risk Prioritization
$2M+
Fraud losses prevented
Business Challenge

A fintech platform faced rising fraud that existing rule-based filters failed to catch, while also blocking 4% of legitimate high-value transactions.

Decision Gap

The risk team made manual review decisions on 600+ flagged transactions daily — unsustainable, with no data layer to prioritize where human judgment was actually needed.

Analysis & Solution

Built a real-time anomaly-detection layer combining transaction and network-graph features, reaching 94% precision at sub-50ms inference, cutting the manual review queue by 70%.

Recommendation

Calibrated the model to accept slightly lower recall to minimize false positives, since blocking a legitimate transaction was more costly to the client's unit economics than missing a low-value fraud event.

Key Insight: Risk models should be calibrated to business economics, not just statistical performance. Matching the model to the client's real cost asymmetry unlocked more value than a generic precision-recall balance would have.

03 — Enterprise SaaS
Commercial Intelligence Dashboard
Power BI Architecture · KPI Design
60%
Faster decision cycles
Business Challenge

A SaaS company's commercial leadership operated on monthly reporting cycles — by the time an insight arrived, the window to act on it had already closed.

Decision Gap

Leaders were flying blind intra-month. Pipeline health, product usage, and expansion signals lived in siloed systems with no unified, real-time view.

Analysis & Solution

Unified a pipeline processing 1M+ events/day into a Power BI intelligence layer with automated anomaly alerts, churn signals, and expansion-opportunity scoring.

Recommendation

Prioritized 12 high-signal metrics over comprehensive reporting, reducing cognitive load and increasing the odds leaders would actually act on what they saw.

Key Insight: The most dangerous dashboard has too many metrics. Ruthless prioritization of what leaders need to decide — not everything they could see — was what drove real adoption.

04 — Logistics / Operations
Automated Data Infrastructure
ETL Architecture · Data Engineering
80%
Less manual processing
Business Challenge

A logistics company's analytics team spent 60% of its bandwidth manually reconciling seven source systems before any analysis could begin.

Decision Gap

Insights arrived 5–7 days after operational events — too late to influence the decisions that generated them. Analytics was acting as a historian, not a decision partner.

Analysis & Solution

Built a cloud-native ETL pipeline on AWS (Lambda + S3 + Airflow) with automated data-quality validation, cutting time-to-insight from 7 days to under 4 hours.

Recommendation

Chose a modular, source-agnostic architecture over a tightly integrated one, so the client could onboard two additional data sources independently after the engagement ended.

Key Insight: Data infrastructure is a strategic asset. The freed analyst capacity funded new analytical work over the following months, built entirely in-house.
